My favorite Tom Cruise movies

Favorites

Here are my 3 favorite Tom Cruise movies and why I chose them:

1. Mission: Impossible III (2006)

Why I chose it: This movie perfectly balances explosive action with a deeply personal and emotional story for Ethan Hunt. Plus, it features one of the best and most intimidating villains in the entire franchise, played brilliantly by Philip Seymour Hoffman.

2. American Made (2017)

Why I chose it: I love seeing a different side of Tom Cruise. Instead of the invincible hero, he plays a charismatic, morally gray pilot and smuggler caught in a wild true story. It is fast-paced, funny, and uniquely entertaining.

3. Edge of Tomorrow (2014)

Why I chose it: The sci-fi time-loop concept is executed masterfully. Watching his character evolve from a cowardly PR officer into an elite frontline warrior makes for incredible character development, backed by spectacular action scenes.
