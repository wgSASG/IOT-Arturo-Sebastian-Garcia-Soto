¿Who is Tom Cruise?

Biography

Thomas Cruise Mapother IV (born July 3, 1962) is an American actor and producer recognized as one of the most successful and highest-grossing movie stars in history. He began his career in the early 1980s, quickly establishing himself as a definitive leading man in Hollywood. He is globally famous for his screen presence, intense work ethic, and dedication to performing his own extreme stunts without the use of stunt doubles.

Important Facts

Full name: THOMAS CRUISE MAPOTHER IV

Origin: Born in Syracuse, New York.

Box office impact: His films have grossed billions of dollars worldwide, making him one of the most profitable actors in the film industry.

Extreme stunts: He is known for learning real, high-risk skills for his movies, such as flying helicopters, performing HALO (High Altitude Low Opening) parachute jumps, holding his breath underwater for over six minutes, and scaling the exterior of the Burj Khalifa.

Awards: He has won three Golden Globe Awards and has received four Academy Award nominations throughout his career.

Career Highlights

Rise to fame (1980s): He achieved worldwide recognition with Risky Business (1983) and cemented his status as a global superstar with the aviation action film Top Gun (1986).

Critical acclaim (1990s): He showcased his dramatic range with Oscar-nominated performances in Born on the Fourth of July (1989), Jerry Maguire (1996), and Magnolia (1999).

The definitive franchise (1996-Present): He is the star and lead producer of the multi-billion dollar action franchise Mission: Impossible, starring as the iconic spy Ethan Hunt.

Modern phenomenon (2022): He reprised his role as Pete Mitchell in Top Gun: Maverick, which surpassed $1.4 billion at the global box office and was widely acclaimed as one of the defining action films of the decade.
